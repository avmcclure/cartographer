Plan layout prototype

Throwaway prototype for issue #20. Can a survey plan be drawn automatically, and do boxes stay put between scans?

What this shows
  • Three sheets drawn by code, with no hand placement: the CIOC system from the sample atlas (real entries), a large made-up system, and a made-up organisation index.
  • Each of the first two sheets has three scans. Between scans a few boxes and links are added or removed.
  • Fresh lets the layout engine (ELK) start again on every scan. Kept positions lays the sheet out once, snaps boxes to a grid, then never moves a surviving box: new boxes go into free space near what they link to, and all lines are drawn again. ELK seeded gives ELK the old positions as a hint, which is the most ELK itself offers. In this trial it moved almost every box anyway and tangled the lines.
  • Orange dotted outlines show where a box was in the previous scan. The numbers under the plan are computed from the drawing.
What is rough
  • ELK in the browser cannot draw lines around boxes that are already placed. The lines in Kept positions come from a crude router written for this prototype: it finds a path on a 10 pixel grid, one line at a time, and does not tidy up afterwards. Expect more crossings and some odd detours.
  • On the real sheet, scans 2 and 3 are invented changes to real entries. The trigger link from NAICS_Example was added by hand, because the sample atlas records triggers in body text and not in links. Total table counts are unknown for the real databases, so only drawn counts are shown.
  • At phone width a whole sheet does not fit at a readable size. Fit shows the shape, 100% lets you read it and scroll.
  • No tests, no polish, no entry pages. Selection follows links downstream only.

Stability readout

Moved counts boxes that are in both scans and are not in the same place. Distances are in grid cells (one lettered square). Crossings, lines through boxes and lines on top of each other are counted from the drawn geometry.
